@title The Last Ember | A Stone Age story
@era ancient
@lang en
@char Gru: male, chief
@char Mara: female
[Scene: cave night | Gru, Mara]
Narrator: Deep in the wild valley, the tribe hides in a cave while wolves howl outside.
Gru (angry): Who let the fire die? The beasts will smell our fear!
Mara (sad): There is no dry wood left, chief.
Gru (thinking): Then I will climb the burning mountain and bring back a flame.
Mara (surprised): Alone? That is madness!
Gru (happy): No, Mara. That is how legends begin.
